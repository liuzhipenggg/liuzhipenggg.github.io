CAPEval

A Decoupled Caption Evaluation across
understanding and generation

CAPEval decomposes caption quality into Coverage (C) and Precision (P) using human-written ground-truth captions and atomic checklists. Empirically, coverage tracks understanding; precision tracks generation.

Zhipeng Liu, Haochen Wang, Zhaoxiang Zhang · arXiv:2608.02589
